ECONM-I · Unit 01 · Growth tools

Two small tools for growth.

A rule-of-70 calculator and a country-growth comparison. Both pair with The "Growth Is Real" briefing and feed the Onboarding Assignment.

tool 01 · rule of 70

How long until the economy doubles?

Slide a growth rate. The rule of 70 estimates how many years it takes for the economy to double in size, and shows how much it grows in a century.

tool 02 · country growth comparison

Pick two countries. Watch the divergence.

Real GDP per capita for a handful of large or instructive economies, 1820 to 2018, in inflation-adjusted dollars. Estimates from the Maddison Project; the data is approximate (especially pre-1900) and is shown on a log scale so curves of different sizes are visually comparable.

Quick check

Pick South Korea and Argentina, then set the starting year to 1960. In 1960 both countries had roughly similar real GDP per capita (around $1,500 to $1,700). By 2018, South Korea is about 38× richer; Argentina is about 13×. Which of the following best explains a divergence like this?
